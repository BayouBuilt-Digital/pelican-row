Booth rent and fees

  • Rent: $175 a month for a standard 10 × 10. Other sizes are available — inquire about sizes and pricing.
  • Commission: 20% of sales

Getting paid

We ring up every sale at the register and pay out once a month, within the first week of the month.

Booth term

  • Length: month to month
  • Notice to leave: 30 days
  • Waiting list: we contact people as space frees up

Looking after your booth

  • Vendors price and tag their own items.
  • Every item should be clearly tagged with your booth number, a short description, and the price.
  • We try not to duplicate booths, so please clear the general type of item you plan to sell with admin first. If there is already a purse booth, for example, we would not want purses sold in the booth beside it.
  • Keep the aisles clear.

Risk and insurance

Vendors sell at their own risk and Pelican Row is not liable. We are not responsible for theft, and vendors are expected to carry their own insurance.
