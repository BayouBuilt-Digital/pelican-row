The market

What you’ll find inside

Pelican Row combines estate pieces with booths from independent vendors in a single storefront. Vendors bring in new items regularly, so the stock changes between visits.

  • Estate pieces

    Furniture, decor, and one-off items sourced from local estates.

  • Vendor booths

    Independent sellers, each running their own booth inside the market.

  • Looking for something specific?

    Tell us what you’re after and we’ll watch for it as new pieces come in.
