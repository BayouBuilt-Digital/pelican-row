About us

Meet James and Brad

Pelican Row grew from one reseller’s stall into a market of more than 100 vendors on Johnston Street in Lafayette.

How we started

Pelican Row began with Brad’s experience reselling at flea markets. After a couple of years, we saw an opportunity to bring a variety of vendors and their unique finds together in one welcoming place. That idea grew into Pelican Row.

A community of vendors

The community responded quickly. Today, more than 100 vendors call Pelican Row home, and there’s a waiting list to join them. As we’ve grown, we’ve opened a whole new upstairs level with even more to explore.

Interested in a booth of your own? Read the vendor hours and terms and send us an inquiry, and we’ll let you know where the waiting list stands.

What you’ll find

Our hope is that every visit feels warm and welcoming, with the fun of discovering something you didn’t expect. We especially enjoy finding rare and unusual pieces, and old sports memorabilia is a favorite.
